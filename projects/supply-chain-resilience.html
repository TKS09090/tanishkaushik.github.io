PROJECT OVERVIEW

The business question

How can a company identify critical supply-chain vulnerabilities, quantify their exposure and prioritize resilience actions before a disruption becomes a business continuity problem?

Objectives

  • Map critical supply-chain nodes and dependencies.
  • Create a transparent risk-scoring framework.
  • Stress-test the network under disruption scenarios.
  • Prioritize practical mitigation strategies.
  • Build a management-friendly monitoring dashboard.

NETWORK VIEW

Illustrative supply-chain network

Supplier → Port → Plant → Distribution → Customer

METHOD

How the analysis works

01

Map

Define nodes, flows, dependencies and critical pathways.

02

Score

Assess probability, impact, exposure and detectability using a consistent scoring logic.

03

Stress-test

Simulate disruption scenarios and quantify operational consequences.

04

Prioritize

Translate risk exposure into mitigation actions, owners and monitoring metrics.
